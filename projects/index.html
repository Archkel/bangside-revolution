Make the work count

  • Keep original project files and dated versions of your work.
  • Describe your own contribution honestly. Do not claim skills or results you cannot demonstrate.
  • Remove passwords, private customer information, and other sensitive data before sharing a project.
  • Agree on payment, responsibilities, intellectual property, and ownership in writing before starting a partnership.
  • Ask for permission before publishing work that belongs to someone else.
